Section 01 Key Operational & Process Pivots to Drive Velocity, Quality, and Value
1
Leadership, Staffing & Hands-On Architecture
Hands-On Functional & Business Architecture: Position Functional and Business Architects as active platform builders who configure directly in the sandbox, enabling real-time decision-making and removing administrative pass-through layers.
Dedicated Workstream Leadership: Align dedicated Senior Solution Leads across all workstreams (Pierre Dufour for Sales, Maria for Book of Business, and Nathan Greer for Servicing) to provide steady architectural direction and empower hands-on delivery.
Proactive Delivery Oversight: Introduce a Senior Manager (20 hpw) to streamline governance, accelerate blocker resolution, and optimize overall meeting cadences.
2
Delivery Process & Ways of Working
Collaborative AI Enablement for Speed: Partner directly with MetLife's AI technical staff to leverage enterprise AI tools (such as Claude and developer copilots) alongside Salesforce tools, driving immediate velocity in story drafting, rapid prototyping, and daily development.
Prototype-First Experience Design: Focus design cycles on working sandbox configurations rather than static wireframes, driving early “shift-left” feedback from end-users during active sprints.
Agile Governance & Forward Refinement: Ensure a disciplined weekly backlog refinement rhythm for upcoming Program Increments (PIs) backed by clear governance—ensuring delivery cadences are consistently followed and risks or blockers are proactively raised well ahead of release dates.
Single Source of Truth in Azure DevOps (ADO): Standardize on ADO as the central hub for all user stories, acceptance criteria, and QA defects to eliminate duplicate tracking and reduce reporting friction.
3
Global Execution & Performance Accountability
Geographic Time-Zone Alignment: Shift target development capacity to Nearshore (LATAM / Mexico) or Onshore to foster real-time, working-hour collaboration and eliminate daily handoff lag.
Data-Driven Program & Individual Metrics: Partner on 30-day joint leadership reviews to monitor both program-level health and individual developer KPIs (tracking story points per sprint, defect leakage, and rework), supporting our shared goal of a 5% PI-over-PI velocity improvement.
Ensure Balanced QA Support: Calibrate QA-to-developer ratios to ensure comprehensive testing coverage across Salesforce, MetLife internal, and TCS delivery streams.
